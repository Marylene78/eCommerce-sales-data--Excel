 E-Commerce Sales Analysis Project
Project Overview
This project involves a deep dive into e-commerce sales data to identify trends, evaluate category performance, and assess regional profitability. The analysis spans three years (2022–2024) and covers various product categories including Electronics, Accessories, and Office supplies.

Key Insights Financial Summary
Total Revenue: $10,667,881

Total Profit: $1,844,665

Overall Profit Margin: Approximately 17.3%.

Category & Product Analysis

Electronics is the dominant category, driving the majority of the profit.

Top Profitable Category: Electronics ($923,185 profit)

Top Individual Product: Camera ($207,631) followed closely by Monitor ($202,028).

Highest Volume: Electronics sold 8,610 units, nearly double the volume of the Office category.

Regional Performance

The West region is the strongest market, leading in both revenue and profitability, while the North region represents the biggest growth opportunity.

Best Performing Region: West ($495,359 profit / $2.84M sales)

Lowest Performing Region: North ($426,315 profit / $2.48M sales)

Data Visualizations Included

The analysis utilized several pivot structures to uncover these "sights":

Category vs. Profit: Identified Cameras and Monitors as high-margin drivers.

Region vs. Profit: Highlighted geographic strengths.

Yearly Trends: Tracked the growth trajectory from 2022 to 2024.

Quantity Analysis: Mapped volume distribution across regions.
